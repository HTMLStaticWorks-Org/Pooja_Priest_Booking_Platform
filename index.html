FREQUENTLY ASKED

Common questions about booking a pooja

🪔 What is included in the pooja package?
+
Our standard booking includes verified priest dakshina, ritual coordination, and pre-ceremony guidance. You can also opt for our "All-Inclusive Samagri" add-on which provides pure A2 cow ghee, fresh flowers, havan samit, camphor, and brass puja essentials delivered right to your home.
📅 Can the priest help calculate the auspicious Muhurtham?
+
Yes, absolutely. When submitting your booking request, simply mention that you need muhurtham guidance. Our senior astrologer acharyas will review your Janma Nakshatra and propose the most auspicious timings based on the Panchangam.
🗣️ Can I choose a priest based on my regional mother tongue?
+
Yes. We have certified pandits proficient in Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, and Sanskrit to ensure your family fully understands the meaning and significance of the sacred mantras.
🏡 Do you perform ceremonies at private venues or only at home?
+
We perform rituals at private residences, apartments, community halls, marriage venues, temple premises, and commercial offices. Simply share your venue address when confirming your booking.
🔄 What if I need to reschedule due to unforeseen circumstances?
+
We understand family schedules change. You can reschedule your ceremony free of charge up to 24 hours prior to the muhurtham date directly from your Devotee Dashboard or by calling our coordinator helpline.
