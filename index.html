FREQUENTLY ASKED

Common questions about booking a pooja

Clear answers to help you plan your ceremony with clarity.

🪔 What is included in the pooja package?

Our standard booking includes verified priest dakshina, ritual coordination, and guidance. You can also opt for our "All-Inclusive Samagri" add-on which provides pure ghee, flowers, havan samit, and puja essentials.

📅 Can the priest help calculate the auspicious Muhurtham?

Yes. When submitting your booking request, simply mention that you need muhurtham guidance. Our senior astrologer acharyas will review your Janma Nakshatra and propose the most auspicious timings.

🗣️ Can I choose a priest based on my mother tongue?

Absolutely. We have pandits proficient in Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, and Sanskrit to ensure your family fully understands the meaning of the rituals.

🏡 Do you support ceremonies at venues or only at home?

We perform rituals at private homes, apartments, community halls, temple venues, and corporate offices. Just provide the venue address during checkout.
