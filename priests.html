How to prepare for your priest's arrival

A few simple steps to ensure your sacred ceremony begins smoothly and auspiciously.

  • Clean Altar Space: Keep the pooja corner or homam space facing East or North-East.
  • Traditional Welcome: Welcome your pandit with water for charanam, a fresh brass thali, and respect.
  • Family Readiness: Family members performing the sankalpam should bathe and wear traditional attire.
  • Samagri Arrangement: Unpack fresh flowers, fruits, and ghee 30 minutes prior to the muhurtham.
