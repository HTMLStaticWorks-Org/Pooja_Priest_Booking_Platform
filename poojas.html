SHASTRA PANCHANGAM

Understanding Muhurtham & Tithi for your ceremony

Choosing an auspicious time (Shubh Muhurtham) aligns cosmic vibrations, planetary positions, and your family's Janma Nakshatra for maximum spiritual benefits.

  • Shukla Paksha (Waxing Moon): Most propitious for Griha Pravesham, Namakaran, and weddings.
  • Brahma Muhurtham (04:30 AM - 06:00 AM): Supreme hour for peace, high energy, and homam.
  • Nakshatra Compatibility: Avoiding Rahu Kalam, Yamagandam, and Ashtami/Navami thithis.
